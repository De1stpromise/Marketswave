About Marketswave

Reimagine wealth creation with diverse markets

For decades, institutions and hedge funds have trusted private markets to grow their portfolios. Marketswave was founded to unlock private markets for more investors than ever before.

Vision

We value continuity, quality and closeness to our clients. Since Marketswave is an independent company where the majority of shares in the company are owned by the proprietors, it is just as important to us, as to you, that the funds perform really well. Together we work hard towards our common goal!

Mission

We have a strong ambition to accelerate the pace towards a sustainable future for people, businesses and society. We want to be a leading catalyst in the sustainability transition. We apply a long-term perspective in everything we do, standing by our customers in good times and in bad, because we know our business is built on relationships and the trust placed in us. The key is our team of skilled employees, and their dedication to providing the best possible customer experience. It's all about creating value through financial products and services that meet our customers' needs in an ever-changing world.

Background & History

We are Marketswave, an independent investment firm specialized in equity-focused and broader market investment solutions.

We offer high-class investment management and strategic advisory for institutions, corporations and private investors.

Our goal is to generate a good return for our clients and shareholders over a longer period of time. Our investment philosophy, based on careful opportunity identification, portfolio optimization and risk-aware strategies, aims to deliver returns clearly above relevant benchmarks where possible.

Every type of investor is welcome to become a client and we have no minimum initial investment.

The relevant investment information, risk disclosures and key documents are available at our website.

We are committed to responsible practices and align with relevant industry standards for transparency and client focus in the financial services sector.
