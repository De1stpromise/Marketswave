Add deposit address

Paste the address from the wallet that will receive client funds. It is checked for format only; whether it is the right address is yours to confirm on the next step.

Confirm this is exactly the address in your wallet. A single wrong character cannot be validated and funds sent to it cannot be recovered.

Assign client

A client holds one address per currency and network. Clients already on another address for this network are listed but cannot be selected — remove them from that address first.

Remove client from address

This is the last client on this address. Removing them retires it — permanently.

A retired address can never be assigned to anyone new. Funds can still arrive at it from a wallet where a client saved it, and reusing it would misattribute them.
