Discretionary Fund Management

Strengthen your investments beyond public markets with a mix of private credit, private equity, and private infrastructure.

Discretionary management lets a qualified team handle the full investment process so you can focus on your life and business rather than day-to-day markets.

$10,000 minimum investment
  • Unlock broad private market exposure with an asset mix designed to perform through market ups and downs. Private equity 40%, Private infrastructure 35%, Private credit 25%.
  • Each asset class carries its own risk profile. Past performance is not indicative of future results. Marketswave reviews all investments for client suitability; if you don't meet the criteria, we'll redirect you to a more appropriate product.
  • Each of the asset classes plays a different role. Private equity focuses on growing businesses, private credit generates income through lending, and infrastructure provides stable, often inflation-linked cash flows.   • With our management we cut down on fees, keeping the portfolio running efficiently at low cost.

Retirement Planning

A simple way to draw down your retirement savings.

Effective retirement planning begins with clear targets and then builds the investment and savings framework required to support them.

$25,000 transfer-fee reimbursement
  • Keep growing, tax-deferred — Your retirement investments keep compounding on a tax-deferred basis. Withdrawals are treated as taxable income when you take them.
  • A structured drawdown lets you keep your nest egg invested and growing while turning it into the income that funds your retirement, without giving up the tax advantages you spent decades building.
  • Required minimum distributions — Most tax-advantaged retirement accounts require you to begin withdrawing by a certain age, with the required amount increasing gradually over time. We help you plan around these thresholds well before they apply.
  • We'll automatically reimburse the transfer-out fees charged by your brokerage when you move at least $25,000 to us.
  • Income splitting for couples — Retirement income can often be shared with a spouse or partner to help lower your household's overall tax bill — one of several strategies we build into your plan.
Operational Resilience

Supply Chain

End-to-end coordination of planning, sourcing, production, delivery, and returns as one integrated system.

A supply chain is a complex logistics system that consists of facilities that convert raw materials into finished products and distribute them to end consumers or end customers, while supply chain management focuses on the optimization of the flow of goods within the supply chain's distribution channels to ensure efficiency.

One integrated system
  • Coordinate planning, sourcing, production, delivery, and returns as one end-to-end system
  • Use timely data and strong forecasting to control inventory, reduce waste, and respond quickly to demand
  • Build dependable supplier relationships while avoiding overreliance on one supplier or region
  • Protect quality through clear standards, supplier oversight, and continuous process improvement
  • Balance efficiency with flexibility and resilience instead of optimizing solely for the lowest inventory or cost
